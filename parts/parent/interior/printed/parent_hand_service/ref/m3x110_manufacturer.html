M3-0.5 x 110 Alloy Steel Metric Socket Head Cap Screw gives maintenance and purchasing teams a metric alloy steel fastener for defined machine locations. Metric sizing helps engineers maintain hardware consistency in globally sourced equipment and metric machine designs. Internal hex drive access supports controlled installation in compact assemblies, service panels, tooling, and automation equipment. The M3-0.5 x 110 size supports exact catalog selection for machine building, tooling, fixtures, automation equipment, and OEM machinery. For engineers, OEM manufacturers, purchasing managers, and maintenance professionals, this alloy steel part helps match the fastener to the assembly without changing the locked dimensions. Robotics, packaging machinery, inspection fixtures, and serviceable machine components are common sourcing contexts for this product family.

Technical Highlights

  • Head Style: Cylindrical socket head, internal hex drive
  • Thread Size: M3-0.5 (coarse metric pitch)
  • Length: 110 mm
  • Head Diameter: 5.50 mm max / 5.20 mm min
  • Head Height: 3.00 mm max / 2.86 mm min
  • Hex Key Size: 2.5 mm
  • Minimum Key Engagement: 1.3 mm
  • Material: Alloy Steel
  • Dimensional Standard: ISO 4762 / DIN 912
